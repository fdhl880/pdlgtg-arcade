## Repeatable procedure

1. Preserve the previous build's actual gameplay recording and source SHA-256 manifest in a numbered evidence directory.
2. Inspect the recording at the starting grid, first bend, anti-gravity climb, hairpin, glider and landing, including short sequences around collisions and camera defects.
3. Compare with real MK8 playback at corresponding sections. Record the source, timestamps and visible differences. Screenshots are evidence of sampled frames, not a claim that every video frame was reviewed.
4. Pick the most conspicuous differences and implement them. Reuse the established phone mapping and race behavior.
5. Record a full race through keyboard events, collect browser errors and timings, inspect the resulting footage, and write a candid pass/reject decision. Staged screenshots are supplementary and explicitly labeled.
6. Apply the bounded acceptance and stopping criteria in DEMO-ITERATION.md. Defer unrelated polish and phone validation. Batch full-race/regression checks after the scoped changes stabilize to conserve quota.
